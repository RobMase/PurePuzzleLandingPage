Why this puzzle exists

As a father of three, I know the problem all too well: you search for a simple jigsaw puzzle for kids — something for a train ride, a waiting room, or just a quiet moment — and you hit a wall of ads, in-app purchases and links to social media.

That's why I built this offline puzzle myself: no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links anywhere outside the app. A puzzle I, as a parent, can hand to my child without worrying about what pops up next.
